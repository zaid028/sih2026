Why Raw Satellite Hotspots Are Not Enough

Raw NASA FIRMS feeds detect thousands of thermal anomalies daily across India. However, authorities face a critical visibility gap: an uncontrolled chemical refinery inferno looks identical in raw coordinates to an authorized blast furnace, a gas flare stack, or agricultural stubble burning.

FIREGUARD AI eliminates this ambiguity by cross-referencing satellite radiative power (FRP) with OpenStreetMap industrial boundaries, multi-week temporal persistence, and an 8-class AI classification model—delivering instantaneous actionable intelligence to NDRF, State Disaster Cells, and plant safety commanders.

End-to-End Operational Data Pipeline

How raw satellite telemetry transitions into frontline emergency response

Step 01
Satellite Ingestion

Ingests MODIS & VIIRS telemetry: latitude, longitude, brightness, and Fire Radiative Power (MW).

Step 02
OSM Spatial Correlator

Performs real-time geospatial buffering against OpenStreetMap industrial polygons and hazard tiers.

Step 03
AI Classification Engine

Evaluates 8 operational classes, temporal recurrence, and calculates an explainable risk score (0-100).

Step 04
Safe Evacuation & Dispatch

Calculates dynamic safe evacuation corridors steering around the 500m danger zone to the nearest hospital.

Tactical Command Capabilities

Built to operational standards used by disaster authorities and facility managers

Interactive Geospatial Command Map

Leaflet.js tactical map with satellite imagery, dark basemaps, pulsing threat markers, industrial asset perimeters, and live coordinate HUD.

8-Class AI Fire Classification

Distinguishes between industrial fires, gas flares, persistent furnaces, forest fires, agricultural burning, waste fires, and solar glint false positives.

Persistent Thermal Detection

Multi-temporal recurrence clustering over days and weeks. Automatically flags abnormal flare spikes when a routine source exceeds 2.5 sigma.

Explainable AI Risk Scoring (0–100)

Transparent causal attribution showing exactly why an incident was designated Low, Moderate, High, or Critical, considering population and lifelines.

Dynamic Evacuation Routing

Generates color-coded safe corridors (Green) that avoid active 500m blast and 1.5km toxic plume perimeters (Red) to the nearest trauma hospital.

Multi-Agency Dispatch Simulation

Constructs standardized emergency alert payloads with copyable coordinates and simulated radio/SMS broadcast to NDRF, Fire Services, and Plant Safety Leads.
